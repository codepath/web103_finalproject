## Reflection

**Draft for team review:** Revise the answers below to reflect the group’s actual experience before submission.

### 1. What went well during this unit?

The initial planning draft establishes TripTogether’s purpose and identifies six core features. The trip-planning idea maps naturally to the required database relationships: trips have multiple activities, and users participate in trips through a membership join table. Writing user stories also helps make the intended user experience more concrete.

### 2. What were some challenges your group faced in this unit?

One planning challenge is keeping the scope manageable while including collaborative features. Trip planning could expand into maps, authentication, and automatic expense settlement, so this draft focuses on trips, activities, voting, and expense records. Team responsibilities and the final feature scope still need to be confirmed.

### 3. What additional support will you need in upcoming units as you continue to work on your final project?

Support with reviewing the PostgreSQL schema and Express API design would help ensure that the database relationships and routes meet the project requirements. Guidance on connecting the React frontend to the backend and deploying the full app on Render would also be useful. A shared task breakdown and agreed deadlines will help coordinate implementation.
