# User Stories

Reference the Writing User Stories final project guide in the course portal for more information about how to complete each of the sections below.

## Outline User Roles

- **College Student Commuter** – A student with a tight budget and limited time who wants to prepare meals ahead of time and avoid buying food on campus.
- **Busy Parent** – A parent managing meals for a household who wants to plan the week's meals, keep track of what's in the pantry, and shop efficiently.
- **Fitness Enthusiast** – Someone who trains regularly and plans meals around nutrition goals, often prepping meals in bulk for the week.

## Draft User Stories

1. As a college student commuter, I want to meal prep for the week, so that I can just grab my lunch in the mornings.
2. As a college student commuter, I want to see recipes I can make with the ingredients I already have, so that I can save money by not buying extra groceries.
3. As a college student commuter, I want to filter recipes by preparation time, so that I can find quick meals that fit around my classes and commute.
4. As a college student commuter, I want to save recipes I like, so that I can easily find and make them again later.
5. As a busy parent, I want to meal prep for the week, so that I can focus on spending time with my family instead of deciding what to cook every night.
6. As a busy parent, I want to add, edit, and delete items in my pantry, so that I always know what ingredients I have at home.
7. As a busy parent, I want to see when ingredients in my pantry are about to expire, so that I can use them before they go bad and reduce food waste.
8. As a busy parent, I want a shopping list automatically generated from the ingredients missing in my meal plan, so that I don't forget anything at the store.
9. As a fitness enthusiast, I want to organize my meals by day in a weekly planner, so that I can bulk prep my meals and stay on track with my nutrition goals.
10. As a fitness enthusiast, I want to filter recipes by dietary preference and meal type, so that I can find high-protein meals that support my training.
11. As a fitness enthusiast, I want to view a recipe's ingredients and instructions in a pop-up without leaving the page, so that I can quickly compare meals while planning my week.
12. As a fitness enthusiast, I want to see which ingredients a recipe needs that I already have and which ones I'm missing, so that I can make the most of my pantry before buying more groceries.
