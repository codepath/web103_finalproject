## Draft User Stories

[👉🏾👉🏾👉🏾 Include at least at least 10 user stories in this format:]

1. As an on-campus student
I want to find  campus activities similar to me so that I can quickly make connections/friends. 

2. New Entry level (Permanent stay possible first time away from home)
As a new entry-level worker, I want to find other early folk just like me so that I am less lonely and can adapt to this new environment quickly.

3. Low-income families (Forced to move be )
As someone from a low-income family, I want to find the cheapest resources, jobs around and available, so that I can be as financially comfortable as possible

4. Group Travelers
As someone in group travel, I want to check the availability of other people so its easier to plan events while I'm in it.

5. Displaced due to natural disasters
As someone displaced, I want to locate come-back programs tailored to victims of displacement so that I can receive cushioning as I try to get my life back together

6. Introverts
As an introvert
I want to:
Find people with similar interest or hobbies
Meet people in smaller groups or one-on-one
So that I can:
Make friends without being overwhelmed by too many people

7.  Extroverts
As an extrovert
I want to:
Meet new people
Explore new hobbies with new people
So that I can:
Become friends with more people
Always have a community of people I can reach out to

8. Niche hobbyist 
As a niche hobbyist
I want to:
Find others that enjoy the same hobby as I do
So that I can:
Form a small community of people I can reach out to about my hobby

9. Local Event planners
As a local event planner
I want to:
Host events for my community based on certain interests
So that I can:
Form a stronger community in the area I live in

10. Recent Graduate
As a recent graduate 
I want to:
Have a way to make new friends consistently
Meet other recent graduates 
So that i can:
Create a meaningful network
